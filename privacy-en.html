Privacy Policy

Papelario app (com.jrsoft.escanerdocumentos) · Last updated: 12 October 2026

In one sentence: Papelario scans, stores and converts your documents inside your phone. There are no accounts and no servers of its own, and your documents, their text and your signatures never leave your phone unless you share them. The only thing that leaves it is technical data from the Google libraries that scan, read the text and serve the ads.

Who is responsible

The data controller is JR Soft, trading name of Javier Román Sáez, developer of the Papelario app. You can get in touch at demual@gmail.com.

What this app does NOT do

  • There is no sign-up, account, username or password for the app. You are never asked for an email address, a phone number, a name or your location.
  • It does not upload your documents. Photos, PDFs, recognized text and your signatures are processed and stored on your phone. This app has no servers of its own.
  • No analytics or usage-statistics tools are used: no Google Analytics, no Firebase Analytics and no crash-reporting services.
  • No data is sold, rented or shared with third parties for commercial purposes.
  • It does not ask for permission to use the camera, your files, your contacts or your location. Photos are taken by Google's document scanner or the system camera, and files are opened and saved through Android's own screens, only the ones you choose.
  • Documents are not included in Android's backup to Google's cloud.

Permissions

  • Fingerprint or face recognition (USE_BIOMETRIC), only if you turn on the app lock. The check is done by Android: the app never receives your fingerprint or your face, only whether it was unlocked or not.
  • Internet, network state and advertising identifier (AD_ID and the permissions of Android's advertising services), which the advertising and the Google libraries need (see below).

Data that stays only on your phone

This information is kept in the app's private storage. It disappears if you uninstall the app, if you clear its data from Android settings or if you use Settings → Delete all my data.

  • Your documents: the original photos of each page, the cropped and filtered pages, and their name, dates, folder and tags.
  • The recognized text of each page (including any text you correct by hand), which is used for searching and goes inside your PDFs.
  • Your saved signatures.
  • The bin: what you delete is kept for 30 days and then removed.
  • Your settings: language, theme, page format, chosen folders, lock and the other options.
  • A PDF or photo you open from another app is copied to the app's temporary storage so it can be imported.

Android backup. The app does not let Android's automatic backup upload your documents, their text, your signatures or your settings to Google's cloud. It does allow the direct phone-to-phone transfer you make yourself when you switch phones (by cable or locally), so you don't lose your library.

What you choose to share

  • Sharing and sending by email. A document (PDF, JPG images, ZIP or Word) only leaves your phone when you share it, with the app you choose on Android's screen. If you add a password, the PDF is encrypted with AES-256.
  • Saving to your phone and the mirror folder. PDFs are saved in the folder you choose on Android's screen. If that folder syncs with a cloud (for example, Google Drive), the syncing is done by the service you chose, under its own terms.
  • Backup. It is saved as a file wherever you choose. If you add a password, it is encrypted (AES-256) and nobody can open it without the password: if you forget it, there is no way to recover it, not even for us.
  • Business cards. “Add to contacts” opens the phone's contacts app with the details already filled in; you are the one who saves them.
  • Clipboard. The app copies text to the clipboard only when you ask it to.
  • Read aloud. The text is read by the text-to-speech engine set up on your phone. If that engine uses online voices, the text is processed by its provider, under its own terms.
  • Report a problem. It opens your email app with a message that includes the app version, the phone model, the Android version and the type of the latest errors (without any of your documents or text). It is only sent if you send it.

Data that does leave your phone

Scanner and text recognition: Google ML Kit

Photos are taken with Google's document scanner and text is recognized with ML Kit, Google libraries that run inside the phone through Google Play services (which download their models the first time). Neither the images nor the text of your documents are sent to Google. What ML Kit does send to Google is technical data about how it is working: the phone's make, model and system version, the app version, a random installation identifier (which, according to Google, is not intended to identify you or your phone), response times and error codes. Google uses this data to diagnose and improve those libraries.

Arabic and Russian text is recognized with Tesseract, an open-source library that is built into the app and does not connect to the internet.

Advertising: Google AdMob

The app is free and is supported by unobtrusive Google AdMob ads, which only appear in the library and on the “Document saved” screen: never on the camera, while you edit or in full screen. Ad requests carry nothing from your documents or their text.

As with any app that uses AdMob, Google's SDK collects the Android advertising identifier (you can reset or delete it in Android Settings → Google → Ads), the app set identifier (app set ID), your IP address (from which an approximate location is inferred), your interactions with the app and with the ads, and diagnostic data. Google uses this data to serve and measure advertising, for statistics and to prevent fraud, under its own policy.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent form before serving personalized advertising. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy options. If you decline, you will see non-personalized advertising and the app works exactly the same.

Ratings and Google Play

Every now and then, after you have used it successfully, the app may ask you for a rating through Google Play's official window, which is managed by Google. “Rate the app” and “More apps by JR Soft” open Google Play.

IP address

As with any app that connects to the internet, the Google services mentioned above receive your IP address when connecting. This app has no servers of its own, so it keeps no logs of any kind.

Legal basis and retention

Processing relies on your consent, which you give by using the app's features and, for personalized advertising, through Google's form, and which you can withdraw at any time from the app itself or from Android settings.

Data stored on your phone remains there until you delete it, uninstall the app or clear its data. This app keeps no data outside your phone; Google keeps its own data according to its policies.

Your rights

If you live in the European Union, the GDPR grants you rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. In practice, and given that this app keeps none of your data outside your phone:

  • Access and portability: share or save your documents as PDF, JPG or Word, or back up your whole library from Settings.
  • Rectification: rename your documents or correct their text in the app itself.
  • Erasure: delete a document, empty the bin or use Settings → Delete all my data; you can also uninstall the app. There is no need to ask anyone.
  • Objection and withdrawal of consent: change your advertising choice in Settings → Ad privacy options.

You can also write to the contact address or lodge a complaint with your country's supervisory authority. In Spain this is the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
